Account help

Continue in Network Tester.

If an account email brought you here, return to the app to check your account and finish signing in.

This page does not confirm your account status or reset your password. Opening an app below does not carry over the sign-in link. If recovery did not open the app, request a new link from that app on the device you want to use.

On your iPhone

Open Network Tester, go to Account, and sign in. For password recovery, choose Forgot password and open the new email on this iPhone.

Open iPhone app

Requires Network Tester to be installed. You can also open it from your Home Screen.

On your Mac

Open Network Tester Companion, go to Account, and sign in. For password recovery, request a new link from the companion and open the email on this Mac.

Open Mac companion

Requires the companion to be installed. You can also open it from Applications.

Email confirmation

Try signing in with your email and password in the app. If it says your email still needs confirmation, follow its instructions or contact support. Seeing this page alone does not mean confirmation succeeded.

A link expired or opened on another device?

Request a fresh password-reset link from the app on your intended device, then open that email there. Use the latest message. For Google accounts, use Google’s account-recovery process.

Need help?

Read account troubleshooting or email support. Describe the error and your app/OS versions. Do not send passwords, email links, sign-in codes, or authentication tokens.

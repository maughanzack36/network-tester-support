Privacy policy

This policy covers Network Tester for iPhone and Network Tester Companion for Mac, provided by Zack Maughan.

Effective October 9, 2026 · Contact: zack.zman99@gmail.com

Before running a WAN test

M-Lab receives your public IP address, measurements, time of the test and diagnostic metadata. Its test data is published publicly and retained indefinitely for Internet research. Declining WAN consent leaves local features available.

1. Local use and companion connection

You can use local features without an account. The iPhone stores saved projects, measurements, settings and consent choices locally. The apps also store credentials needed to remember accounts and paired companions.

Pairing exchanges connection and authentication information between your devices. LAN tests exchange synthetic test traffic and diagnostics with your gateway or the paired Mac. Network observations may include device/interface names, IP addresses, interface or neighbor MAC addresses, link information and timing. These observations can identify devices on your network.

Saved network details can be included in optional cloud synchronization. Exported CSV/PDF files may contain project names, addresses, notes, measurements and selected network details. You decide where to save or share exports.

2. Optional accounts and cloud synchronization

Email/password and Google sign-in use Supabase authentication. We process your account identifier, email and available name/profile information to sign you in, associate data with your account and synchronize projects. Google handles its own authorization; the app does not receive your Google password.

If you enter or upload them, your profile may include a phone number and photo. Synced data can include project/room names, site addresses, notes, measurements, dates and optional network details. Supabase hosts account records, project data and private profile-image storage. Cloud data is associated with your account; signing in with a different account may show a different set of projects.

These data support authentication, synchronization, profile display, troubleshooting and operation of the service. The apps do not include advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking.

3. Internet tests and external services

M-Lab

WAN speed tests connect to Measurement Lab (M-Lab), including NDT7 or the multi-connection test mode. M-Lab receives speed/latency measurements, the public IP used for the connection, timestamps and diagnostic environment information. Its test data is public, retained indefinitely and available for Internet research and other uses by third parties. The app does not send your Supabase account identifier, project notes or profile photo as part of an M-Lab test.

Account deletion or consent withdrawal in these apps does not erase published M-Lab results. For M-Lab’s data practices and requests about its data, see M-Lab’s privacy policy.

Provider lookup and connectivity checks

After WAN consent, provider lookups contact IPinfo to display Internet-provider information. IPinfo sees the connection’s public IP and request information. See IPinfo’s privacy policy for its handling of that information.

Network diagnostics may also contact DNS, Internet connectivity or test destinations to evaluate reachability and response time. Those destinations necessarily see the source IP and connection requests. Project content is not intentionally sent in these probes. Internet and cloud services may process requests outside your state or country.

Service providers

Authentication, hosting and email providers may process connection information and operational/security logs to deliver and protect their services. Relevant policies include Supabase and Google. Provider policies supplement this policy; they do not replace our responsibilities for the app’s data.

4. Permissions and security

Local-network access allows discovery and communication with your companion and LAN devices. Choosing a profile photo shares the image you select. File-export access lets the Mac write to a destination you choose. The apps do not request system location permission, although an IP address can reveal an approximate location.

Companion control and HTTP transfers use TLS and paired authentication. Native iPerf throughput uses authenticated setup followed by ordinary unencrypted test payload traffic. Account/cloud connections use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage provides absolute security.

5. Retention and deletion

Local saved data remains until you remove it or the app’s stored data. Cloud account and project data is retained while your account exists. Deleting an individual synced measurement marks it deleted; this is different from deleting the account and does not necessarily immediately remove the underlying database record.

Use Account → Delete Account in either app to request permanent account deletion. After the server confirms success, the account and associated cloud profile, projects, measurements and avatar are removed, and the local account session is cleared. A failed request is reported so you can retry.

Account deletion does not remove exported files, another device’s unsynchronized guest projects or published M-Lab data. iPhone deletion can separately include local guest projects when that option is offered. Other devices may retain local copies until they reconnect or you remove their app data.

Operational logs, service-provider backups and support correspondence are separate from live account records and may remain according to provider retention processes or applicable obligations. We do not promise an immediate purge from every backup. Contact us for current retention details or a data request.

6. Your controls and requests

  • Decline WAN consent to avoid M-Lab tests and provider lookups.
  • In WAN settings, choose Withdraw WAN consent to stop an active WAN test and provider lookup. You will be asked again before another WAN test. iPhone consent is scoped to the current account or guest on that device; Mac consent is scoped to that installation.
  • Use iPhone account data/sync controls to manage automatic sync and optional network details. Disabling a setting does not retroactively erase earlier uploads.
  • Export saved data, delete records, sign out, or delete your account in-app.
  • Email us for access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you; do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

7. This website and support messages

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, cookies or browser-storage features added by us. GitHub processes visitor request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and attachments through our email provider. We use them to respond, troubleshoot and maintain relevant support records. Please redact private project data, passwords, tokens and unnecessary network identifiers before sending diagnostics.

8. Updates and contact

We update this page when practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes requiring a new choice will be reflected in the apps. Questions or requests: zack.zman99@gmail.com.
